Director — fashion, luxury & beauty films

Mathieu Missiaen directs short films for fashion, luxury and beauty houses — shot with motion control, simulated in CGI, or both.

Films include the Hermès Bolide campaign, Dior One Essential, Audemars Piguet Royal Oak Openwork, Kenzo Move Revolution, Nike TN La Requin and several pieces for Loewe, from runway launches to the Loewe × Studio Ghibli collaboration.

Every film is available on the Moving image page.

Réalisateur à Paris : films de mode, de luxe et de beauté, motion control, motion design et animation 3D pour Hermès, Dior, Loewe, Kenzo, Audemars Piguet, Nike.
