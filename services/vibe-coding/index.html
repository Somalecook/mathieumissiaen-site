Vibe coding — apps, tools & websites built with AI

Mathieu Missiaen vibe codes: he designs and ships software by working with AI, the way an art director works with a team. The result is real, published work.

Stickoo is his language-learning app for children — words collected as stickers, in English, French, Spanish, Korean, Japanese and Chinese. He also built this portfolio website, a rashguard customiser and other small creative tools.

For brands, the same approach makes interactive campaign pieces, prototypes and microsites fast to try and easy to change.
