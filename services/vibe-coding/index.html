Vibe coding — AI-assisted apps, tools & websites

Mathieu Missiaen vibe codes: he designs and ships software with AI assistance, the way an art director works with a team. The result is real, published work.

Stickoo is his language-learning app for children — words collected as stickers, in English, French, Spanish, Korean, Japanese and Chinese. He also built this portfolio website and other small creative tools.

For brands, the same approach makes interactive campaign pieces, prototypes and microsites fast to try and easy to change.
