Photography · Paris

Still life & campaign photographer in Paris

Mathieu Missiaen is a still life and campaign photographer based in Paris. Trained in set design, he builds what he shoots: objects, liquids, flowers and products lit like portraits.

He art-directed and photographed the worldwide Kenzo Move campaign, an editorial series on the Rolls-Royce Dawn, fragrance and beauty still lifes, and the Acne Paper 30th anniversary images shown in Acne Studios windows worldwide.

Photography and CGI often meet in the same image: a real set, extended or rebuilt in 3D.

Photographe nature morte et campagne à Paris : mode, luxe, beauté, parfum. Direction artistique, décor et prise de vue pour Kenzo, Rolls-Royce, Acne Paper.
