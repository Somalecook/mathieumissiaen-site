Branded games & apps · Paris

Branded games & educational games — game and app developer for brands

Mathieu Missiaen creates games for brands. As a creative director and app developer, he designs, illustrates and develops branded games, advergames and educational games himself — from the idea to the published game.

Games for brands: Yongi (용이) is the branded game he made for iOLO, the Paris ceramics brand — a free virtual pet game in the browser where a little pink dragon grows in a hand-painted garden, iOLO ceramics appear on his shelf, and ten perfect days unlock a discount in the iOLO shop. A brand universe people come back to every day.

Educational games: Stickoo is his language-learning game for children — first words collected as stickers in English, French, Spanish, Korean, Japanese and Chinese, published on Android and iOS.

For brands and agencies he makes branded games, advergames, gamified campaigns, interactive campaign pieces, prototypes and microsites — art direction, illustration, CGI and code under one roof, fast to try and easy to change.

Créateur de jeux pour les marques : jeux de marque (branded games), advergames, gamification de campagnes et jeux éducatifs, conçus et développés de A à Z par un directeur créatif parisien — dont Yongi, le jeu de la marque iOLO, et Stickoo, un jeu pour apprendre les langues aux enfants.
