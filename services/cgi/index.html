CGI & 3D · Paris

CGI artist & 3D art director in Paris

Mathieu Missiaen is a Paris-based CGI artist and 3D art director. He builds photoreal images, objects and simulations in Houdini and Cinema 4D — liquids, glass, frozen fruit, materials that behave like the real thing — and takes them from first sketch to final render.

Recent CGI work includes global campaigns with Wieden+Kennedy Tokyo for Amazon Japan / Prime Video and Suntory -196, backlit images shown at Art Basel for La Prairie, and product content for Loewe.

The same tools drive his personal research: masks rebuilt in Houdini, procedural generators, custom lens shaders. Commissions get both the art direction and the hands-on CGI execution.
