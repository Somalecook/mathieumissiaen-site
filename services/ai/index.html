AI image making & generative workflows

Mathieu Missiaen uses generative AI the way he uses a camera or Houdini: as one more tool in a controlled, art-directed pipeline. Node-based workflows, his own references and 3D scans keep the result specific instead of generic.

His research on masks mixes hand-made sculpture, photography, Houdini and generative tools, and he explores models trained on his own images. For brands, AI is used for exploration, look development and moving tests — always finished with real craft.

IA générative et workflows nodaux au service de l'image : recherche, look development, tests animés, toujours combinés à la photo et à la 3D pour un résultat sur mesure.
