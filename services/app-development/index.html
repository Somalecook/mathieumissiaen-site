App developer — apps, games, creative tools & websites

Mathieu Missiaen is also an app developer: he designs and ships software with AI assistance, the way an art director works with a team. The result is real, published work.

Stickoo is his language-learning app for children — words collected as stickers, in English, French, Spanish, Korean, Japanese and Chinese. He also made Yongi (용이), a free virtual pet game for iOLO: a little pink dragon to raise in a hand-painted garden. And this portfolio website, and other small creative tools.

For brands, the same approach makes interactive campaign pieces, prototypes and microsites fast to try and easy to change.
